Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account we collect your name, email address and password. As you use the service, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect financial account numbers or connect to your bank.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and protected with reasonable security measures. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may delete your expenses or request deletion of your account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the support address provided on our website.
